Challenge

The campaign had two major challenges: working within FIFA’s strict guidelines, and bringing two very different brands together into one successful 360 campaign.

Constraints

Every asset had to pass two tests. It had to follow FIFA’s marketing guidelines, with correct logo lockups and colors, and it had to follow Motorola’s new rebrand guidelines.

Motorola provided new brand guidelines for FIFA, so the team was also exploring how the new identity should work under FIFA’s rules for the first time.

Solution

We explored ways to display the FIFA × Verizon collaboration: FIFA’s color schemes carried the partnership, while Motorola’s new brand identity led in social support formats.
